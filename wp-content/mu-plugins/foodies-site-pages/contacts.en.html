We are the Foodies Academy team: cooks, recipe authors and photographers. We are happy to hear your questions about recipes, ideas for new dishes and partnership proposals.

How to reach us

  • Telegram — @foodies_academy
  • Instagram — foodies.academy
  • Facebook — Foodies.Academy
  • YouTube — Foodies Academy: our video recipes; you can ask questions in the comments.

What to write about

  • A question about a recipe — the easiest way is to leave a review under the recipe, so other readers see the answer too.
  • A mistake on the site or in a recipe — send us the link to the page and tell us what is wrong.
  • Partnerships and advertising — tell us about your project and the format you have in mind.
  • Authors — if you would like to publish your recipes on Foodies Academy, send us a couple of examples with photos.
  • Your data — to see, correct or delete your data, delete your account or unsubscribe (see our privacy policy).
