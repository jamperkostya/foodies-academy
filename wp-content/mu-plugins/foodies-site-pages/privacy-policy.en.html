Effective from 21 November 2026.

Foodies Academy (foodies.academy) is a website of recipes from around the world. This page explains what data we collect, why, where it is kept and how you can control it. We collect only what the site needs to work and we never sell data to third parties.

What we collect

Your account

To register you give your name, e-mail address and a password. The password is stored only in encrypted form — we cannot see it. Your account keeps your favourite recipes and the authors you follow. Once you log in, your browser keeps a sign-in key in its storage (localStorage) so you are not asked for the password on every page.

Reviews and comments

A review is published on the site with the name and rating you give. To fight spam we also keep the IP address it was sent from; it is never shown on the site. All reviews are moderated.

Newsletter and recipe book

To subscribe you give your name and e-mail address. We use them only to send the newsletter or the recipe book you signed up for. You can unsubscribe with the link in any e-mail or by writing to us.

Likes, views and search

Likes and views of recipes, videos and articles are counted without linking them to you: your browser only remembers in localStorage that you have already voted or opened the page today. Searches made on the site are stored anonymously so we can see which recipes people look for.

Settings in your browser

Your browser also keeps the number of servings and the measurement system (metric or US) you chose, your answer about cookies and your progress through a recipe's steps. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics and cookies

To understand which recipes our readers like, we use Google Analytics (Google) and Yandex Metrica (Yandex). These services set cookies and receive information about your visit that does not identify you by name: pages viewed, where you came from, your device and approximate location. Yandex Metrica may record interactions with a page (Session Replay) without the contents of input fields.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, these counters load only after you accept them in the cookie banner. You can also opt out in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on and the Yandex Metrica opt-out add-on.

YouTube videos

Our video recipes are hosted on YouTube. The player loads only when you click a video; from then on Google's privacy policy applies to it.

Where data is kept and who receives it

Account, review and subscription data is kept on our own server. We do not sell it or share it with third parties, apart from the analytics and video services above and where the law requires it.

We keep your data for as long as your account or subscription exists. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica under their own terms; using them may involve transfers of data outside the UK and the EU.

Your rights

At any time you can:

  • ask what data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected, or have your account, reviews and subscription deleted;
  • withdraw your consent to analytics by clearing the site's cookies — the banner will then ask you again;
  • unsubscribe from the newsletter.

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can also restrict processing, receive your data in a machine-readable form and complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

To use these rights, write to us — see Contacts. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 16 to register. If you learn that a child has registered without their parents' consent, write to us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the way the site works changes. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
